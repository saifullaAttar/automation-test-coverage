What this measures. Coverage here is TestMO cases that have at least one automated test mapped to them, against automation_web_2.0 main. Every link was checked by reading the test's docstring against the case's steps; the Evidence column on each row states what is and is not covered.

What it does not measure. It is not a pass rate — it says nothing about whether these tests currently pass. “Covered” counts fully covered plus partially covered cases; on the strict reading of fully covered only, the figure is . Arabic is AR-ready at the locator level and has not been run on BrowserStack on either platform.

Why these differ from the 31 Aug review. That review put mWeb at 44% (16/36) and app at 55% (16/29). Its mWeb denominator was a different TestMO group whose cases turned out to be empty placeholders; this report measures the regression repository (group 73697) instead. The app denominator is identical — 29 — and the increase there is re-mapping: the store-credit, order-summary, wishlist and gift-registry tests were previously linked to the wrong cases or to none. Neither figure was moved to look better; both are reproducible with make generate.
